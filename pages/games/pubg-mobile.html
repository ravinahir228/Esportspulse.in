GAME OVERVIEW

PUBG MOBILE Overview

PUBG MOBILE is a mobile battle royale game built around competitive survival, tactical gameplay, teamwork and large-scale multiplayer combat.

This EsportsPulse hub will bring together verified information about the game's development, gameplay, updates, esports ecosystem, tournaments, teams, players and major competitive events.

01

Development

Development and collaboration behind PUBG MOBILE.

Development

Development and collaboration behind PUBG MOBILE. Tap to read more.

PUBG MOBILE was developed through a collaboration between PUBG Studios and Tencent Games. The goal was to bring the original PUBG PC experience to smartphones while maintaining realistic gameplay and large-scale battle royale combat.

Tencent's Lightspeed & Quantum Studio optimized the game for mobile devices, introducing smooth controls, improved performance and cross-region server infrastructure capable of supporting millions of players worldwide.

Since its launch, PUBG MOBILE has received hundreds of updates, new maps, weapons, vehicles, modes and esports features, making it one of the most successful mobile games in history.

02

Global Launch

PUBG MOBILE launched globally in March 2018. Tap to read more.

PUBG MOBILE officially launched worldwide on March 19, 2018. Developed by Tencent Games in collaboration with PUBG Studios, it brought the PC battle royale experience to mobile devices.

Within months, PUBG MOBILE became one of the most downloaded mobile games globally, reaching millions of players across Asia, Europe, North America, South America and the Middle East.

The game introduced Erangel, Miramar and Sanhok, realistic gunplay, squad battles and 100-player survival matches that changed mobile gaming.

03

Competitive Era

The game developed into a global competitive esports ecosystem. Tap to read more.

As PUBG MOBILE grew in popularity, it quickly evolved from a casual mobile game into one of the world's largest esports titles.

Regional leagues were introduced across Asia, Europe, Middle East, Africa and the Americas, creating a structured competitive pathway for professional players and organizations.

Major tournaments such as PMCO, PMPL, PMSL, PMGC and PMWC helped establish a global esports ecosystem with multi-million dollar prize pools and worldwide audiences.

The competitive scene produced legendary teams, star players and historic rivalries, making PUBG MOBILE one of the most watched mobile esports games in history.

04

Global Esports

International tournaments, professional teams and players became a major part of the ecosystem. Tap to read more.

PUBG MOBILE esports expanded into a truly global competitive ecosystem with professional leagues, regional championships and international tournaments.

Events such as PMGC (PUBG MOBILE Global Championship), PMWC (PUBG MOBILE World Cup) and PMGO (PUBG MOBILE Global Open) attract the best teams from around the world.

Organizations from India, Brazil, Indonesia, China, Turkey, Europe, Middle East and North America compete for international titles and multi-million dollar prize pools.

Today, PUBG MOBILE esports is watched by millions of fans globally and remains one of the largest mobile esports ecosystems ever created.
